THE TRAVELER'S SCROLL

Frequently asked questions.

How do I create my account?

In the "Create your account" section of this page. Accounts can no longer be created from the game: if you type a username that doesn't exist, the game will say the account or password is incorrect.

Where do I set my password?

When you create your account on this page. It's the same one you use to log in to the game. Keep it safe: we can't recover it.

What do I download?

The full client (just once) and the launcher. The launcher goes inside the game folder and keeps it up to date.

Do I have to download the client again for every update?

No. The launcher downloads only what changed and checks that it arrived complete.

Do I need to install anything else?

No. It works on Windows 10 and 11 without installing anything extra.

Is this the official Angels Online website?

No. It's a community website for this server, not affiliated with the game's publisher.
